HANDOVER SPEC (DETAILED)

Topic: Refine marginal VWAP acceptance from hard suppression to selective delay
1. Context (what the previous version proved)
What v0.8.1.10.0 introduced

v0.8.1.10.0 added a marginal-day VWAP acceptance guard that requires two prior completed candles (i-2 and i-1) to both:

close green, and

close above VWAP,

before allowing a marginal-day entry.

The rule was applied only when:

DAY_GATE / day-follow-through is enabled,

the day is classified as marginal (close_gt_vwap_count == 1),

the marginal trade is still available (day_trade_count < 1),

and entry is otherwise eligible.

The rule was implemented as an early reject (MARGINAL_VWAP_ACCEPT_FAIL) inserted between:

the existing DAY_GATE_FAILED early reject, and

the combined entry condition (strat.should_enter(...)).

No other behavior was changed.

2. What v0.8.1.10.0 conclusively demonstrated (do not re-litigate)
A/B results (two independent months)

April 2025

A (v0.8.1.9.0):
trades=24, losses=14, totalPnL=-209.89

B (v0.8.1.10.0):
trades=17, losses=9, totalPnL=-91.27

Improvement: +118.62

August 2025

A (v0.8.1.9.0):
trades=18, losses=12, totalPnL=-251.80

B (v0.8.1.10.0):
trades=11, losses=6, totalPnL=-70.08

Improvement: +181.72

Across both months:

~7 trades removed

losses reduced far more than wins

win rate materially improved

drawdown collapsed

This establishes that marginal VWAP acceptance gating removes a regime-independent failure class.

Loss attribution (April deep dive)

Five full-stop SL losses present in v0.8.1.9.0 were entirely removed in v0.8.1.10.0:

GRI (2025-04-01)

IBO (2025-04-04)

NAOV (2025-04-08)

BON (2025-04-11)

CNEY (2025-04-24)

All five were:

early 09:30 marginal entries,

explicitly blocked by
EARLY_REJECT reason=MARGINAL_VWAP_ACCEPT_FAIL,

and never traded later that day.

Therefore, v0.8.1.10.0 acted as a hard suppressor for these symbols — and that behavior was correct for these cases.

TWCS counter-example (critical nuance)

JNVR 2025-04-07:

Was suppressed entirely by v0.8.1.10.0.

TWCS confirms it was a legitimate marginal continuation:

strong VWAP slope,

multiple green candles above VWAP,

clean +2.0% TP within one minute.

This establishes that:

The concept (VWAP acceptance before marginal entry) is correct.

The shape of the rule (“i-2 AND i-1 must both close above VWAP”) is too brittle and can suppress valid delayed acceptance patterns.

3. Core problem statement for v0.8.1.11.0

v0.8.1.10.0 correctly removes early marginal junk but does so via hard suppression, not selective delay.

The next version must:

Preserve the removal of early 09:30 marginal losses (GRI/IBO/NAOV/BON/CNEY class),

Restore legitimate marginal winners that show real VWAP acceptance slightly later (JNVR class),

Without re-introducing early marginal SLs.

4. Single hypothesis for v0.8.1.11.0 (do not add more)

Hypothesis:
Marginal entries should require confirmed VWAP acceptance within a short recent window, not strictly consecutive candles immediately before entry.

The failure in v0.8.1.10.0 is not lack of acceptance — it is over-constraining acceptance timing.

9. Hand-off summary (one paragraph, quotable)

v0.8.1.10.0 validated that requiring VWAP acceptance before marginal entries materially reduces losses across regimes, but its strict “two consecutive prior closes above VWAP” requirement acts as a hard suppressor and can block legitimate delayed continuations.
v0.8.1.11.0 should refine the acceptance condition to preserve early-loss suppression while allowing delayed VWAP acceptance patterns, using a single, minimal rule change validated across April and August with TWCS confirmation.
