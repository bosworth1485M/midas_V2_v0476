## 3. Feature Planned for Next Version (v0.4.8)
**Green-Candle + 1-Second Confirmation (Micro Plug-in)**  
Validates that:
1. Three strong-body green 1-minute candles occur before entry.  
2. 1-second candles confirm continuation after the final minute.  

Expected effect: filter weak momentum trades and increase expectancy.
