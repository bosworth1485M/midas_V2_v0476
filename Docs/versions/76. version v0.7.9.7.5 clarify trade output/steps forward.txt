# Parameter Atlas – Scenario B (v0.7.9.7.5)
## Use this to understand exactly what we are trading.

## 1. Scanner / Universe (topgappers)

- price_min: 1.0
- price_max: 20.0
- min_gap_pct: 10.0
- top: 5

Where it lives today:
- Implemented in scripts/topgappers.py and related scanner logic.
- Logged as `price=[1.0..20.0]  min_gap=10.0%` and “Trimmed to Top-5 symbols”.

## 2. Strategy (SimpleBreakoutStrategy – Scenario B)

- gate_minutes: 20
    - Must wait 20 minutes after open before any entry.
- min_pm_vol: 30,000
    - At least 30k shares traded premarket.
- min_rvol_open: 2.0
- rvol_open_minutes: 15
    - In first 15 minutes, today’s volume ≥ 2× yesterday’s volume.
- rise_bars: 3
- green_body_min: 0.22
    - Require 3 consecutive green candles, each with strong body (≥ 22% of the bar).
- require_macd_rise: true
- macd_rise_bars: 2
    - MACD histogram > 0 and rising for the last 2 bars.
- tp_pct: 2.0
- sl_pct: 2.5
    - Take profit at +2.0%, stop loss at –2.5%.
- dip_reclaim: false
    - Dip-reclaim mode off in this profile.

Where it lives today:
- strategy.py → StrategyParams, loaded via norm_params.
- Values confirmed in the “RULES WE USED BEFORE TAKING THIS TRADE” block and the strategy_params snapshot.

## 3. Risk / Safety

- per_trade_risk (effective): ≈ 35 USD (for B-tier in this branch)
- max_trades_per_symbol: 1
- daily_max_loss: 1000.0

Where it lives today:
- Risk sizing / settings passed into backtester.
- Observed through SIZE logs and risk_snapshot.
- Daily cap shown in “RISK CALCULATION” and in risk_snapshot.

## 4. Summary – What we are trading (Scenario B, v0.7.9.7.5)

Scenario B currently trades:
- Small-cap gappers priced between $1 and $20, with at least 10% gap, Top-5 by gap.
- Waits 20 minutes after open, and requires strong premarket activity (≥ 30k shares).
- Requires strong opening volume (RVOL ≥ 2.0 in first 15 minutes).
- Requires 3 consecutive strong green candles before entry.
- Requires MACD histogram above zero and rising for 2 bars.
- Uses TP +2.0% and SL –2.5%.
- Risks around $35 per trade, with 1 trade per symbol and a $1000 daily max loss.

Step 3 — Plan candle snapshot logging against this schema

Because each trade already has:

entry_time

exit_time

symbol

date

and soon a DB row,

you can design a simple candle snapshot scheme:

For each trade, save:

minute bars from, say, 10 minutes before entry to 10 minutes after exit;

second bars from, say, 60 seconds before entry to 60 seconds after entry.

Store:

Either the bars in a trade_candles table, or

PNG files with filenames like:
candles/minute/2025-08-01_B_MWYN_entry.png
candles/second/2025-08-01_B_MWYN_entry.png

and keep paths/keys in the DB row.

Because the DB knows the exact rules used for each trade, you’ll later be able to ask:

and adjust knobs with eyes open.
