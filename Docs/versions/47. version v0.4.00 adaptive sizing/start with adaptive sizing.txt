⚖️ The Core Problem: High WR but Negative Expectancy

Expectancy per trade ≈
(WinRate × AvgWin) – ((1 – WinRate) × AvgLoss)

You can have a 70 % win rate and still lose money if:

Your average loss is > your average win, or

You’re allocating the same size to every trade (so bad trades erase good ones).

🧩 How the Profit-Max Plan Fixes It
1️⃣ Adaptive Position Sizing (Confidence-Weighted Risk)

Effect: Increases AvgWin × WinRate relative to AvgLoss.
High-probability setups (A-grade catalysts, strong RVOL, clean PMH reclaim) get 1.5–2 × normal size, while marginal setups get 0.5 ×.
Losing trades shrink, winning trades compound.

🔹 Impact: Converts a 70 % WR / −0.1 R system into +0.2 R or higher.
It’s the single most powerful fix for your symptom.

2️⃣ Opening RVOL Gate

Effect: Removes low-momentum trades that pad win rate but have tiny follow-through.
Your August data already showed “0 / 3” or “small-PnL” days on low-RVOL symbols — this gate cuts those.

🔹 Impact: Reduces trade count but raises average win and PnL consistency.

3️⃣ Catalyst Score ≥ 3 + Polarity Filter

Effect: Keeps only fundamentally stronger catalysts (earnings beats, FDA wins, contract awards).
These produce wider post-entry expansions, meaning your average win % grows even if WR stays constant.

🔹 Impact: Expectancy improves because the win-side R multiple widens.

4️⃣ Support/Resistance-Aware Reclaim Logic

Effect: Avoids chasing extended breakouts; instead enters on pullbacks to VWAP/EMA/PMH where risk is tight.
This lowers average loss per trade without changing WR.

🔹 Impact: Loss-side term in expectancy equation shrinks → higher net expectancy.

5️⃣ Profit-Guard (Partial + BE Move)

Effect: Locks in small wins before they reverse — converting “small red” or “flat” trades into +0.2–0.3 R.
You stop giving back half of your winners.

🔹 Impact: Raises overall average win and tightens distribution of losses.
