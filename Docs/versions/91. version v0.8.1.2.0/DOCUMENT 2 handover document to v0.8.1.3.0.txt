1. Why this handover exists

This handover follows v0.8.1.2.0, which was a documentation-only version created to record and reject a payoff-geometry experiment (tightening stop-loss).

No production code changes were adopted in v0.8.1.2.0.

This document therefore serves one purpose:

To clearly define what is now frozen, what has been ruled out, and what single new knob should be explored next to lift profitability.

2. Current confirmed baseline (DO NOT CHANGE)

The following configuration is explicitly frozen and must be treated as the baseline going into the next version.

Scenario B — Gap-and-Go (Baseline)

Take-profit: +2.0%

Stop-loss: −2.5%

VWAP Extension Gate: ON

vwap_extension_gate = true

vwap_extension_max_pct = 1.5

Gate minutes: 20

RVOL open filter:

min_rvol_open = 2.0

rvol_open_minutes = 15

MACD rising bars: 2

Green candle requirement: 3 bars, body ≥ green_body_min

Max trades per symbol: 1

No catalysts

No adaptive sizing

No partial exits

No trailing stops

No microstructure expansion or pressure gating

This baseline was re-verified after the experiment via a single-day run on 2025-08-06 and behaved as expected.

3. What was tested and ruled out (important context)
Tested in v0.8.1.2.0

Stop-loss tightening: 2.5% → 2.0%

Geometry only; no other changes

Outcome

Win rate collapsed

Volatility increased

Net PnL worsened materially

Baseline SL 2.5% clearly superior

Explicit conclusion

Payoff geometry is not the primary profitability lever for Scenario B at this stage.

This conclusion is now considered settled and should not be revisited unless the entry model itself changes materially.

4. What NOT to work on next (explicit exclusions)

The following areas are intentionally deferred and must not be included in the next version:

Further SL / TP tuning

Adaptive SL / TP by trade quality

Partial profit-taking

Trailing stops

Adaptive sizing

Catalyst-based filtering

Complex microstructure logic

Multi-knob experimentation

Reason:
All of these either:

Mask the real failure mode, or

Add complexity before the core problem is addressed.

5. The real problem identified

From the controlled experiments and logs:

Good days (e.g. 2025-08-06):

Strategy performs well

VWAP-disciplined entries succeed

Bad days (e.g. 2025-08-07, 2025-08-08):

Multiple trades meet entry criteria

Price fails to follow through

Losses cluster, regardless of geometry

Core insight

Scenario B loses money primarily by participating on the wrong days — not by managing individual trades poorly.

This reframes the optimization target.

6. Required shift in focus

The next improvement must operate at the day level, not the trade level.

Instead of asking:

“How do we manage trades better?”

We must ask:

“When should we not trade at all?”

This is a structural shift in control logic.

7. Recommended next knob: Day Follow-Through Gate
Concept (high-level)

Introduce an optional day-level gate that determines whether Scenario B is allowed to trade at all on a given day.

If early market behavior does not show real momentum follow-through:

Scenario B is disabled for the remainder of the day

No trades are taken, regardless of individual setups

This mirrors discretionary Cameron-style behavior:

“If nothing is working today, stand down.”

8. Why this knob is the correct next step

This knob is recommended because it:

Directly targets the largest source of losses (bad days)

Can eliminate entire −100 to −200 PnL days

Preserves behavior on strong momentum days

Is orthogonal to VWAP, MACD, RVOL, SL/TP

Does not depend on catalysts

Is simple, explainable, and testable

Fits the “single-knob, single-version” discipline

9. Design principles for the next version

The next version must follow these strict principles:

One new knob only

Binary decision per day (trade vs no-trade)

Optional and configurable (OFF by default initially)

Evaluated once per day, not per trade

Explicit WHY logging for pass/fail

No interaction with trade management logic

No stacking, no tuning sprees.

10. Candidate knob definition (conceptual)
New Scenario B parameters (conceptual)

require_day_follow_through

day_follow_through_minutes

day_follow_through_min_symbols

Conceptual behavior

After day_follow_through_minutes from market open:

Examine the top-N gappers already in the universe

Count how many symbols show real follow-through, defined by at least one simple condition:

Price holding above VWAP

OR price exceeding opening-range high

OR strong green candle with meaningful body

If the count is below threshold:

Mark day as FAILED

Disable Scenario B trading for the rest of the session

11. Logging and observability requirements

The gate must produce day-level WHY logs, such as:

DAY_GATE: CHECK

DAY_GATE: PASSED symbols=3

DAY_GATE: FAILED symbols=1 reason=insufficient_follow_through

These logs must appear:

Once per day

Before any trade decisions

In both passing and failing cases

This is critical for later TWCS and UI analysis.

12. Success criteria for the next version

The next version is considered successful if, with the gate enabled:

Worst-day losses are materially reduced

Average daily PnL improves

Trade count on strong days remains similar

Strategy behavior remains explainable and stable

Failure criteria:

No measurable reduction in bad-day damage

Excessive suppression of good-day trades

13. Relationship to future roadmap

This knob is intentionally placed before:

Catalysts

Adaptive sizing

Confidence-tiering

Microstructure refinements

Those features become more powerful after bad days are filtered out.
