# v0.3.21 Plan — Gold Standard Filters

## Scope
- **Catalyst quality filter:** require A or better (`--min-grade 2`), no B-fill by default.
- **Magnitude-aware scoring:** keep %→score mapping (A/A+/super-A).
- **Negative headlines:** already excluded (drop-neg).
- **Denylist hygiene:** drop warrants (`*.WS`, `-WT`, etc.) and optional China ADRs.
- **Gap & price band:** keep Cameron bands (`price 1–20`, `gap 10–40%`).

## Tape Confirmation (essential for WR)
- **Opening RVOL gate (default 1.5):** require first 10–15m volume ≥1.5× prior day same window.
- **Rising green candles (MANDATORY):** require 3 consecutive green 1m bars after the gate.
- **Optional:** `--rise-with-volume` → green candles must also show rising volume.
- **EMA + VWAP confirm:** require reclaim and slope ≥ threshold.
- **MACD confirm:** line > signal and histogram rising (last 2 bars).

## Entry & Risk
- **One trade per symbol** (for now).
- **Hard risk:** per-trade SL (2.5%) and daily max-loss cap.
- **Time windows:** entries allowed in morning session + power hour, optional mid-day off.
- **Spread/liquidity guard:** skip if bid-ask spread too wide or last-1m volume below floor.

## Sizing Framework
- **Score-based sizing (log only in v0.3.21):**
  - A = 1.0× risk
  - A+ = 1.5× risk
  - Super-A = 2.0× risk
  - All capped by per-trade and daily limits.

## Reproducibility & UX
- **Summarizer footer:** print min_grade, topn, rvol gate, rise-bars, rise-with-volume.
- **One-pager summaries** remain the single source of truth.
- **SAFE runner echo:** print selected filters at run start.
