That’s a really good, honest concern — and it’s one that has come up in every Cameron-style project I’ve supported. Let me give you a structured comparison so you know where you stand.

1. Typical Early Pass Rates

In the first few versions (v0.1–0.3 in your case), almost all projects had poor pass rates — sometimes <40%.

This is normal: before MACD histogram checks, rising green bars, RVOL gates, and catalyst hygiene, the filter set is too loose and trades include a lot of junk setups.

2. What Turned Them Around

The projects that eventually became successful (60–65% win rate, ~$500+/day) shared a sequence of upgrades you are now starting to implement:

Stricter confirms:

MACD line above signal plus histogram rising 2–3 bars.

EMA slope + VWAP reclaim required.

Rising green candles enforced.

Catalyst hygiene:

A-grade catalysts (score ≥2) became mandatory.

B-grade used only to fill Top-N when the list was too small.

Volume gates:

Premarket volume ≥30k.

Opening RVOL gate (≥1.5× vs prior day) within first 10–15 minutes.

Risk-perceived sizing:

Bet more when odds are stronger (multiple confirms + A-grade catalyst).

Still cap daily loss (e.g. $1k).

Timeframe precision:

Entries moved from 1-minute candles → 1-second candles.

1m still used for trend confirm, 1s for entry.

Router integration:

Multiple scenarios (D strict, E dip) combined with precedence rules, one trade per ticker.
