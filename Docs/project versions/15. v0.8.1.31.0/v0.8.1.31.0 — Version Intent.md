v0.8.1.31.0 — Version Intent (paste at top of new thread)

Intent:
This version implements the next step in the Project Status / Cameron Alignment Plan: correcting over-broad structural damage gating that is causing excessive zero-trade days.

Evidence from v0.8.1.30.0 showed that disabling ASC_GREEN removed a blocking guard but did not materially change participation on zero-trade ranges; instead, trades were primarily blocked by POST_DAMAGE_ENTRY_LOCKOUT and STRUCT_DAMAGE_FAIL.

The goal of this version is not to add new entries, indicators, or features, and not to optimize win rate. The sole objective is to scope structural damage lockouts more narrowly, so they behave like Cameron’s contextual damage rules rather than acting as global symbol kill-switches.

Success criteria for this version are:

Fewer zero-trade days in Scenario B on historically “good” momentum regimes

No re-introduction of previously identified catastrophic loss clusters

Clear, explainable A/B separation versus the prior version

Only one structural rule will be adjusted. All other behavior remains unchanged.
